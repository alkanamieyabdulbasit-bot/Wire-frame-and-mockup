Campus Companion

A mobile app that puts a student's timetable, campus map, events and profile in one place. Designed for Bayero University Kano.

Designer: AbdulBasit AbdulAzeez Alkanamiey · 200 level, Software Engineering · Bayero University Kano
Designed with AI assistance (Claude). See "Use of AI assistance" in the last tab.

Try the prototype

Tap the tab bar to move between screens. Tap a weekday on Timetable (only Friday has a class), tap Save on Events, and tap "View on map" on Home.

Brief: help students find their next class, the building it is in, and what is happening on campus, in as few taps as possible.

Design annotations

Home

The next class comes first because it is what students check most often. It shows time, room and building so no second lookup is needed. "Coming up" gives a glance at events without leaving the screen.

Timetable

Organised by day with a week strip, so a student sees one day at a time. Days with no classes show an empty state instead of a blank screen.

Campus map

The selected building sits in a card under the map with a single clear action, "Get directions". The map is illustrative; a live map would replace it in a real build.

Events

Filter chips (All, Today, Clubs) narrow a scrollable feed. Each card has a Save action so students can keep events for later in Profile.

Profile

A simple settings list with identity at the top. Sign out is visually separated and outlined so it is not tapped by accident.

Overall choices

Consistency: one tab bar on every screen, so users always know where they are. Restraint: a single blue accent keeps the app calm, academic and easy to scan. Accessibility: dark text on light surfaces for strong contrast, and large tap targets of at least 44px height.
